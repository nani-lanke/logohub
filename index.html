Welcome to LogoHub! This page is like a big wall of company logos. Each logo lives inside a coloured box. When you see a logo you like, just click on it — and it will take you straight to that company's website! No typing, no searching. Just one click and you're there.

Each box has a coloured border around it. The colour tells you what kind of company it is — even before you click. For example, blue means Technology, pink means Entertainment, and teal means Healthcare. This makes it easy and fast to find exactly what you're looking for.

Want to add your own company or YouTube channel? Fill in the simple form on the Add Icon page. After a quick review, your logo will appear here too!

How It Works

The page shows lots of boxes in a grid. Every box has a number so you can find it easily. When a company puts their logo in a box, that box belongs to them. Click the logo — a pop-up appears with the company's name, type, and links. Boxes without a logo are empty and still waiting for a company to join!

What the Colours Mean

Each logo has a coloured border. This is your colour guide — it tells you what the company does at a glance. A blue border means it's a tech company. A green border means food or travel. A pink border means entertainment. This way you can quickly spot the type of company you want — even before clicking the logo!

LogoHub is a simple and fun way to explore companies from all over. One click takes you to any company's homepage. The coloured borders help you see what kind of company it is right away. Whether you're curious, doing research, or just browsing — LogoHub makes it quick, easy, and enjoyable for everyone.
