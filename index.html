How it works

Three steps. One click.

  1. 01

    Spot a logo

    Every box on the wall has a number. The coloured border tells you the type of company before you click.

  2. 02

    Click it

    A pop-up opens with the company's name, what they do, and buttons for each of their links.

  3. 03

    You're there

    One more tap takes you straight to their website, YouTube channel, Instagram or WhatsApp.

The grid

Every box has a home

The page shows lots of boxes in a grid. Every box has a number so you can find it easily. When a company puts their logo in a box, that box belongs to them. Boxes without a logo are still empty and waiting for a company to join.
